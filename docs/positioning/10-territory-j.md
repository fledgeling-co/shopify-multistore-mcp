## 1 · The position

Buyers of online stores are told not to trust seller screenshots, and diligence leans on screen-shares and temporary read-only access [C-006]. With a seller's approval, shopify-multi-mcp can connect a candidate store read-only, so the buyer's assistant asks it questions directly and compares several targets in a row [T-006] [T-007].

**Hero sentence:** *Look inside the stores you might buy, read-only.* `[T-006]` `[T-007]` `[C-006]`

## 2 · Who it is for

**Beachhead:** portfolio buyers and brokers evaluating several Shopify stores in the same quarter. `[T-006]` `[C-006]`

**What they spend on instead today:** screen-share sessions, seller CSV exports, third-party diligence providers, Flippa Premium [C-006].
**Explicitly not for:** agencies, merchants with their own stores.

## 4 · The word and the enemy (Ries & Trout)

- **Own:** **`read-only`** — the property a seller needs before granting access.
  **Who owns it today:** Shopify staff accounts with limited permissions · **What it would take to take it:** a seller-side approval flow designed for deals.
- **Enemy:** **seller-supplied screenshots** — buyer guides tell readers not to trust them [C-006].

## 5 · Category and naming

**Adopt:** read-only diligence for store buyers.
**Avoid:** "verification" (this does not verify profit, ad spend or supplier costs).
**Ten-second test:** "Before you buy a store, ask it questions yourself instead of trusting the seller's screenshots."
**Never:** all-in-one, everything-app, or any breadth-led framing.

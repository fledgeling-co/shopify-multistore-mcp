## 1 · The position

Merchants who run two to six of their own stores — regional storefronts, a D2C and a wholesale store — compare them by exporting from each admin and reconciling in a spreadsheet [C-005]. shopify-multi-mcp lets their AI assistant ask every store the same question and lays the answers side by side, each labelled [T-007] [T-002].

**Hero sentence:** *Ask all your stores one question. Read the answers side by side.* `[T-007]` `[T-002]` `[C-005]`

## 2 · Who it is for

**Beachhead:** owners and operators of 2–6 of their own Shopify stores (Plus expansion stores or grouped organizations) who already use an AI assistant. `[T-001]` `[C-005]`
**Where they congregate:** r/shopify, Shopify Plus community events, sync-app review pages.
**What they spend on instead today:** sync and reporting apps (Matrixify, Syncio, Multi-Store Sync Power) and Shopify's own Plus multi-store analytics [C-005].
**Explicitly not for:** agencies (Territory A's beachhead) and single-store merchants.

## 4 · The word and the enemy (Ries & Trout)

- **Own:** **`side-by-side`** — the shape of the answer, concrete and easy to demo.
  **Who owns it today:** Shopify's own multi-store analytics, for numbers it already reports · **What it would take to take it:** proof on questions those dashboards cannot answer.
- **Enemy:** **the export-and-paste spreadsheet** — the workaround merchants describe for comparing stores [C-005].

## 5 · Category and naming

**Adopt:** cross-store answers for your own stores.
**Avoid:** "dashboard" (Shopify and sync apps own it), "sync" (implies writing between stores, which this does not do).
**Ten-second test:** "Ask your AI how the UK store is doing next to AU, and get both answers in one reply."
**Never:** all-in-one, everything-app, or any breadth-led framing.

## 6 · Blue Ocean — ERRC

| Action | Factor | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Eliminate** | Per-store CSV exports for a comparison | The comparison runs where the question is asked [T-007] |
| **Reduce** | Monthly app spend for occasional comparisons | Free and MIT [T-009] |
| **Raise** | Ability to ask an unplanned question across stores | A dashboard answers only what it was built to show |

**The empty square this opens:** ad hoc cross-store questions without a reporting app.
**Candidate tagline:** *All your stores, one question.*

## 10 · Evidence fit, risks, honesty

- **Supported by:** C-005 (medium).
- **Disconfirmed or complicated by:** no merchant was found saying an AI assistant's one-store limit is the problem; their voiced pain is catalog matching.
- **Contested claims this rests on:** none.
- **Not yet claimable:** T-012 (real-store sign-in verified).
- **Defensibility:** low; Shopify's analytics and reporting apps sit closer to this buyer.
- **Biggest risk + mitigation:** the answer is not trusted because products and currencies differ across stores. Mitigation: show each store's own numbers, never a merged total.
- **What would falsify this territory:** interviewed owners of several stores preferring their analytics dashboard to an assistant answer on 4 of 5 real questions.

## What this could not establish

How many merchants run 2–6 stores and use an AI assistant; Shopify does not publish an organization count.
